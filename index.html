About this explorer

How to use it

Pick a topic, then open the Walkthrough tab to watch the attack or defense unfold step by step. Add up to three topics to the compare tray to see them side by side. Use the quiz and incident drills to check what stuck.

What it covers

Twenty-four topics across people and identity, web apps, networks, endpoints and malware, and security strategy. Each topic maps to the confidentiality, integrity and availability triad and, where one exists, a public reference such as MITRE ATT&CK, OWASP or NIST.

Accuracy and limits

Walkthroughs are simplified for learning and real incidents vary. This is not a replacement for formal training, vendor documentation or your organization’s policies. Only practice offensive techniques on systems you own or are authorized to test.
